Identity model and simulator retry update

The analysis uses the original identity-link binomial model. The logistic
fallback has been removed. Reported model predictions are clipped to 0-100%;
coefficients and percentage-point effects are unchanged. Clipping does not
repair an unsuitable experimental design or guarantee valid inference at
the probability boundaries.

Both experiment types now retry missing simulator results at the configured
timeout (30 seconds by default). Each retry is logged, reloads the exact
prepared attacker and defender inputs, and repeats the same batch. Failed
attempts do not advance the experiment or write a CSV result. Transient page
readiness failures during condition loading also retry. Retries continue
until success or Stop; unrelated failures still surface normally.

Validation: 25 automated tests passed, including probability bounds, retry
timeouts, cancellation, and exact input restoration. The supplied 90-lineup
defender experiment completed analysis with the identity model. Retry tests
used simulated website failures; no live simulation batches were submitted.
